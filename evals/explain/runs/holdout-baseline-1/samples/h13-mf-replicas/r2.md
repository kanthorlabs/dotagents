`ashgrove-portal` runs **7 replicas** in production.

- **Base:** `k8s/base/deployment.yaml:6` sets `replicas: 2`.
- **Prod overlay:** `k8s/overlays/prod/replicas.yaml:6` patches that to `replicas: 7`. `k8s/overlays/prod/kustomization.yaml` applies the patch.
- **Staging, for comparison:** `k8s/overlays/staging/replicas.yaml` sets 3.

The directory has no autoscaler manifest. The 7 is the declared count in these files. A HorizontalPodAutoscaler defined elsewhere in the cluster could change the live count.
