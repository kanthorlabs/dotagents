The proposal fixes the stall, but as written it lets an old reading overwrite a newer one in `latest_reading`. Aelita recommends approving it only with a guard on `UPSERT_LATEST`, which costs one `WHERE` clause.

**Existing behaviour**

1. `ingest/handler.py` calls `with_retries(save_reading)` for each message.
2. `ingest/retry.py` sleeps 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 seconds after each `TransientError`. That is 62 s in total.
3. `deploy/consumer.yaml` sets `max_ack_pending: 1`, so the consumer handles one message at a time.
4. The sleep therefore blocks every other device. On 26 October there were 17 such stalls of 62 s each.
5. The Timescale failover at 10:14 pushed lag to 41,200 messages, and it took until 10:31:10 to drain.

**Timeline without the fix** (the proposal as written)

1. `freezer-7` sends reading A at 10:00:00 with -20 °C. `ingest` receives it.
2. `save_reading` raises `TransientError` on A. The handler calls `nak(delay=2)`.
3. Redeliveries fail three more times with delays of 4, 8 and 16 s. A now waits until about 10:00:30.
4. `freezer-7` sends reading B at 10:00:30 with -10 °C. With `max_ack_pending: 256`, `ingest` processes B at once and saves it. `latest_reading` shows -10 °C.
5. JetStream redelivers A at about 10:00:31. `save_reading` succeeds, and `UPSERT_LATEST` sets `value = EXCLUDED.value` with no check on `read_at`.
6. `latest_reading` goes back to -20 °C with A's older `read_at`. `freezer-temp-high` stops counting the warming, and the live map shows a stale value.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 5.

5. JetStream redelivers A at about 10:00:31. The upsert carries `WHERE latest_reading.read_at < EXCLUDED.read_at`. A's `read_at` is older, so the update does nothing.
6. `latest_reading` keeps -10 °C. `readings` still gets A, and reports order it by `read_at`.

**What changes**

- `ingest/store.py`: `UPSERT_LATEST` gets `WHERE latest_reading.read_at < EXCLUDED.read_at` after the `DO UPDATE SET` clause. This is an add.
- `ingest/retry.py` and `ingest/handler.py`: delete them and the `with_retries` call, as proposed.
- `deploy/consumer.yaml`: `max_ack_pending: 1` becomes `256`, and `max_deliver: 1` becomes `6`.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends approving Ulrich's proposal with the guard. The root cause is that the upsert trusts arrival order. The guard makes `latest_reading` correct for any delivery order, and the rest of the proposal gives the stall fix. What can break:

- A lost ack after a commit now causes a redelivery, because `max_deliver` rises above 1. That inserts a duplicate row into `readings`. I found no unique key in the repository.
- The proposal handles only `TransientError`. Today any exception goes to the DLQ. Under the proposal, another exception leaves the message unacked until `ack_wait` expires, and it is redelivered after 90 s.

**Alternative: keep `max_ack_pending: 1`.** JetStream redelivery with a single in-flight message preserves order, so no guard is needed. The failing reading still blocks the others during the nak delay, so the stall goal is not met. It becomes the primary only if the guard cannot ship today.
