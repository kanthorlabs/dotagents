Pointing `osprey-nightly-report` at `pg-osprey-2` breaks the job today, because the job writes twice and the hot standby rejects every write. Aelita recommends a read-only churn query plus a separate primary connection for `record_run`. That moves the heavy reads to the replica and keeps one small insert on the primary.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. `osprey-nightly-report` starts at 01:00 UTC. `DATABASE_URL` now comes from secret `osprey-replica`, so it connects to `pg-osprey-2`.
2. `revenue_by_region` runs a plain `SELECT`. The replica serves it.
3. `churn_by_plan` runs `CREATE TEMP TABLE churn_window` in `reports/queries.py`. The hot standby rejects it.
4. `main` in `jobs/nightly_report.py` raises. `write_pdf` and `record_run` never run. No PDF exists and `report_runs` has no row for the day.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 3.

3. `churn_by_plan` runs one `SELECT` with a CTE in place of the temp table. The replica serves it.
4. `write_pdf` writes `nightly-<day>.pdf`.
5. `record_run` opens a second connection to `pg-osprey-1` and inserts into `report_runs`. The insert takes milliseconds.
6. The primary sees one insert at 01:00 instead of 22 minutes of reporting reads. The CPU spike in `logs/primary-load-2026-10-26.txt` should go away.

**What changes**

- `deploy/cronjob.yaml`: `DATABASE_URL` switches from `osprey-primary` to `osprey-replica`. Add `PRIMARY_DATABASE_URL` from `osprey-primary`, key `url`.
- `reports/queries.py`: `CHURN_WINDOW` and `CHURN_BY_PLAN` merge into one `WITH churn_window AS (...) SELECT ...`. `churn_by_plan` runs one statement.
- `jobs/nightly_report.py`: `main` opens the replica connection for the two queries. It opens a primary connection only for `record_run`.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends the split, because the replica must stay read-only and `report_runs` lives on the primary. It costs one extra secret and one extra connection. Two things can break it. A missing `PRIMARY_DATABASE_URL` fails the job after the PDF exists. A failed insert leaves a PDF with no run row.

Replica lag is not a risk here. The p99 is 2 seconds, and the job reads yesterday's data at 01:00 UTC.

**Alternative: keep the job on the primary and tune the queries.** It needs no new secret and no code split. It costs the same primary load, since the 88% CPU spike stays. It becomes the primary if the team will not give the job write access through a second secret.
