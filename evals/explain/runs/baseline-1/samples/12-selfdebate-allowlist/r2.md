`plugin_env` in `runner/spawn.py` copies the whole runner environment into every plugin and removes only the names on `DENYLIST`. Aelita recommends an allowlist instead of adding two names to the denylist. Your denylist edit fixes these two keys and leaves the next secret exposed.

**Existing behaviour**

1. `plugin_env` starts from `dict(os.environ)`.
2. It pops the three names in `DENYLIST`: `AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `DATABASE_URL`, `GITHUB_TOKEN`.
3. It adds `TINKERBOX_PLUGIN_DIR` and passes the result to the plugin.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. On 2 October the team adds `STRIPE_API_KEY` to `deploy/runner.env` for the billing worker. Nobody updates `DENYLIST`.
2. A customer sends a `feed-fetch` request with `debug` set.
3. `run_plugin` spawns `plugins/feed-fetch/main.py` with `STRIPE_API_KEY`, `SENTRY_DSN` and `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET` still in its environment.
4. `feed-fetch/main.py:14` prints `dict(os.environ)` to stderr.
5. The customer pastes that stderr into a public support ticket.
6. Selin Aydin reports INC-57 on 24 October. `STRIPE_API_KEY` is rotated.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 3.

3. `run_plugin` builds the environment from an allowlist. `STRIPE_API_KEY`, `SENTRY_DSN` and `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET` never reach `feed-fetch`.
4. `feed-fetch/main.py:14` prints only the allowed variables.
5. The ticket holds no secret.
6. A later secret in `deploy/runner.env` is also blocked by default.

**What changes**

- `runner/spawn.py` old behaviour: `plugin_env` copies `os.environ` and pops `DENYLIST` names. New behaviour: `plugin_env` copies only `PATH`, `HOME`, `LANG`, `HTTPS_PROXY` and every name that starts with `TINKERBOX_`. `DENYLIST` is deleted.
- `docs/plugin-contract.md` add one sentence: "A plugin sees only `PATH`, `HOME`, `LANG`, `HTTPS_PROXY` and `TINKERBOX_*`."

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends the allowlist. It removes the root cause: a new secret leaks until someone remembers to denylist it. `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET` already sits in `deploy/runner.env` and is missing from your proposed edit. It leaks the same way today. Each allowlisted name has a checked consumer:
- `plugins/feed-fetch/main.py:9` reads `HTTPS_PROXY`.
- `plugins/pdf-stamp/main.py:8` reads `HOME`.
- `plugins/csv-clean/main.py:5` reads `TINKERBOX_CSV_DELIMITER`.

`PATH` and `LANG` are needed by Python and subprocesses. A future plugin that needs another variable breaks until that name is allowlisted or renamed to `TINKERBOX_*`.

**Alternative: your denylist edit plus `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.** It is a three-line change and risks no plugin breakage. It becomes the primary only if you must ship before checking plugins outside this repository, since an unknown plugin may need other variables.

**Also open:** `feed-fetch` dumps its whole environment under `debug`. The allowlist makes that harmless, but you could still remove the dump. That follows after this ruling.
