**Next open item: the retry limit.** `handle_bounce` retries a bounced mail 12 times, but `smtp-mx-2` blocks the sender after 5 bounces to one recipient in one hour. Aelita recommends lowering `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT` to 3.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. `invoice-mailer` sends INV-2207 to Oskar Brandt through `smtp-mx-2`. His mailbox no longer exists, so the mail bounces once.
2. `handle_bounce` sees attempt 0, below `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT` of 12. It waits 60 seconds and resends. The second bounce arrives.
3. The same cycle repeats every 60 seconds. The sixth bounce arrives about 5 minutes after the first.
4. `smtp-mx-2` sees more than 5 bounces for one recipient within one hour. It blocks the sender for 24 hours.
5. `handle_bounce` keeps retrying until attempt 12. `smtp-mx-2` refuses every retry.
6. Every other invoice from `invoices@mail.larkspur.io` waits 24 hours. One dead mailbox delays all customers.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 2.

2. `handle_bounce` sees attempt 0, below the new limit of 3. It waits 60 seconds and resends. The second bounce arrives.
3. The cycle repeats twice more. The fourth bounce arrives.
4. `handle_bounce` sees attempt 3 and calls `message.mark_failed()`. Four bounces stay below the threshold of more than 5. Other invoices keep flowing.

**What changes**

- `mailer/config.py`: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT` changes from `12` to `3`.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends the config change because it forbids the dangerous case and adds no mechanism. Four bounces leave one spare for a second mail to the same recipient within the hour. A soft bounce from a busy server now fails after about 3 minutes instead of 12. Whether `attempt` starts at 0 or 1 is unverified, because the caller of `handle_bounce` is not in these files. A limit of 3 is safe either way.

**Alternative: tell hard bounces from soft bounces.** `handle_bounce` fails a hard bounce at once and retries only soft bounces. It removes the root cause, which is retrying a mailbox that does not exist. It costs code that classifies SMTP reply codes, which `docs/mail-provider.md` says does not exist today. It becomes the primary when many recipients have dead mailboxes.
