**Ruling recorded:** invoice-mailer moves to `invoices@mail.larkspur.io`. The project has no decision register or handoff file, so I did not write to disk. `FROM_ADDRESS` in `mailer/config.py` still reads `billing@larkspur.io` and changes with the move. Two items remain after this one.

`invoice-mailer` gets blocked on `smtp-mx-2` for 24 hours because `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT = 12` allows more than 5 bounces to one recipient within an hour. Aelita recommends a limit of 4.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. `invoice-mailer` sends INV-2207 to Oskar Brandt through `smtp-mx-2`. His mailbox is full, so bounce 1 comes back.
2. `handle_bounce` waits 60 seconds and retries. Bounce 2 comes back.
3. `handle_bounce` retries every 60 seconds. Bounces 3 to 5 arrive by minute 4.
4. The retry at minute 5 bounces. This is bounce 6 within one hour, so `smtp-mx-2` blocks the sender.
5. `handle_bounce` keeps retrying until attempt 12. `smtp-mx-2` rejects every retry.
6. For 24 hours, no invoice from `invoice-mailer` reaches any customer.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 4.

4. Bounce 5 arrives and `handle_bounce` sees attempt 4. It calls `mark_failed()` and sends no sixth mail. `smtp-mx-2` does not block the sender.
5. Invoices to other customers keep flowing. Oskar Brandt's invoice sits in state failed for a person to follow up.

**What changes**

- `mailer/config.py` old value: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT = 12`. New value: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT = 4`.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends the limit of 4. The 5-bounce cap per recipient and hour is a rule of `smtp-mx-2`, so a config value is the smallest change that respects it. The cost is that a recipient with a briefly full mailbox fails sooner. The attempt counter is not visible in any file here, and limit 4 stays at or under 5 bounces whether it starts at 0 or 1. The 60-second interval stays, because 5 sends then fit well inside one hour.

**Alternative: tell hard bounces from soft bounces.** `handle_bounce` fails a hard bounce at once and retries only soft bounces. It removes wasted retries to dead mailboxes. It costs new bounce parsing, and `docs/mail-provider.md` says none exists today. It still needs the limit of 4 for soft bounces. It becomes the primary when dead mailboxes cause most of the bounces.
