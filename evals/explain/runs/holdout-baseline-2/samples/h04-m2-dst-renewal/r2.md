`kestrel-billing` charged 212 subscribers twice because Europe/Berlin clocks fell back on 25 October, so 02:30 happened twice and `charge_renewals` fired at both. Aelita recommends moving the three schedules to UTC. It costs a one-hour shift in local fire time twice a year.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. `schedules/charge_renewals.yaml` sets cron `30 2 * * *` in `Europe/Berlin`. `scheduler/cron.py` `matches` compares the cron to local wall-clock time only.
2. At 00:30 UTC (02:30+0200) `scheduler/loop.py` fires `charge_renewals`. `billing/renewals.py` charges 212 subscriptions with `renews_on <= today`.
3. `send_dunning` fires at 00:45 UTC and sends 41 emails.
4. At 01:00 UTC Berlin clocks fall back from 03:00 to 02:00.
5. At 01:30 UTC (02:30+0100) `matches` is true again. `due_jobs` in `scheduler/clock.py` only checks a 60-second gap, and the last fire was 60 minutes earlier, so the job fires again.
6. `charge_renewals` selects the same 212 subscriptions. `settle_renewals` has not run, so `renews_on` has not moved. `psp.charge` in `billing/psp.py` sends no idempotency key, so Payrail accepts every charge.
7. `send_dunning` also fires a second time and sends the 41 emails again.
8. `settle_renewals` runs at 04:00 UTC and settles 424 `renewal_charges` rows. 212 customers are charged twice, and several get two dunning emails.

**What changes**

- `schedules/charge_renewals.yaml`: `cron: "30 2 * * *"` becomes `"30 0 * * *"`, and `timezone: Europe/Berlin` becomes `UTC`.
- `schedules/send_dunning.yaml`: `"45 2 * * *"` becomes `"45 0 * * *"`, and the timezone becomes `UTC`.
- `schedules/settle_renewals.yaml`: `"0 5 * * *"` becomes `"0 4 * * *"`, and the timezone becomes `UTC`.
- `docs/scheduler.md`: add the rule "Every job uses `timezone: UTC`. Local timezones are forbidden."

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends UTC for all three schedules. UTC has no repeated or skipped hour, so no job can fire twice on a clock change. It also fixes the dunning duplicate, and the spring-forward day, when 02:30 does not exist and the job would be skipped. The cost is that jobs run at 02:30 Berlin in summer and 01:30 Berlin in winter. The run date passed to `charge_renewals` stays the Berlin date, because 00:30 UTC is always the same calendar day in Berlin. It does not stop a double fire from a scheduler restart inside the matching minute, because `last_fired` lives in memory.

**Alternative: idempotency key in `psp.charge`.** Build the key from `sub.id` and `renews_on`, which stay stable until `settle_renewals` runs. It protects against any double fire, including a restart, and it costs a change to the `psp.charge` signature. It becomes the primary if the restart risk matters more than removing the DST cause. It does not stop the duplicate dunning emails.
