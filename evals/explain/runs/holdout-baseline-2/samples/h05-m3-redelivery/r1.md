**What changes**

- `ingest/store.py` `UPSERT_LATEST`: add `WHERE latest_reading.read_at < EXCLUDED.read_at` after the `DO UPDATE SET` clause. Old behaviour: it always overwrites. New behaviour: it overwrites only when the incoming reading is newer.
