`ashgrove-portal` runs **7 replicas** in production.

- **Base:** `k8s/base/deployment.yaml:6` sets `replicas: 2`.
- **Prod overlay:** `k8s/overlays/prod/replicas.yaml:6` patches it to `replicas: 7`. `k8s/overlays/prod/kustomization.yaml` applies that patch to the base.
- **Staging, for contrast:** it patches the count to 3.

I found no autoscaler in the directory, so nothing here changes the count at runtime. A cluster-side autoscaler that isn't in these files could still do so.
