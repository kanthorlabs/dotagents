Aelita recommends that you do not drop `legacy_sku` now. `v_partner_feed` reads it, and `partner_feed_export` sends it to Oakline Supply as `partner_ref`. Your grep of `src/` missed the view, the job and the importer.

**Existing behaviour**

1. `db/views.sql` defines `v_partner_feed` with `COALESCE(p.legacy_sku, p.sku) AS partner_ref`.
2. `jobs/partner_feed_export.py` selects `partner_ref` from that view every night.
3. The job uploads the CSV to `sftp.oaklinesupply.com`.
4. Oakline Supply keys its catalog on the Brightstock code, which only `legacy_sku` stores. `docs/history/2024-brightstock-migration.md` says so.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. You run `ALTER TABLE products DROP COLUMN legacy_sku`. Postgres rejects it because `v_partner_feed` depends on the column.
2. Someone adds `CASCADE`. Postgres drops `v_partner_feed` silently.
3. The nightly `partner_feed_export` runs `SELECT ... FROM v_partner_feed`. The query fails and no feed reaches Oakline Supply.
4. Suppose instead someone rewrites the view as `p.sku AS partner_ref`. The job runs, but the 6,140 migrated products now carry new codes.
5. Oakline Supply cannot match those codes to its catalog. Its prices and items for them go stale or get rejected.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 1.

1. You keep `legacy_sku` and run no migration.
2. `v_partner_feed` and `partner_feed_export` run as before. Oakline Supply keeps matching its codes.

**What changes**

- `db/schema.sql`: no change. The column stays.
- `db/views.sql`: no change.
- `importers/brightstock_import.py`: no change. It also names the column, so it would break on a rerun after a drop. It ran once, on 12 March 2024.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends keeping the column. It is the only store of the Brightstock codes, and a partner outside this repository depends on them. The cost is one nullable text column. Nothing breaks.

**Alternative: re-key Oakline Supply first, then drop.** Oakline Supply moves its catalog to `sku`. You then change the view to `p.sku AS partner_ref`, drop the column and delete the importer. It removes the dead column for good. It costs a coordinated change with an external partner. It becomes the primary when Oakline Supply agrees to re-key.
