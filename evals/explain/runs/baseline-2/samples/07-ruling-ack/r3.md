**What changes**

- `mailer/config.py` old behaviour: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT = 12`. New behaviour: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT = 4`.
- `mailer/bounces.py` stays unchanged. Its check `attempt >= config.BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT` already enforces the new limit.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends the lower limit. It is a one-line edit and it removes the cause, which is a retry count above the relay cap. The cost is that a soft bounce, such as a full mailbox, gets 4 retries over 4 minutes and then fails. Other invoices to the same recipient in the same hour also count toward the cap of 5. A recipient with several open invoices can still trigger a block.

**Alternative: tell hard bounces from soft bounces.** `handle_bounce` marks a hard bounce failed at once and retries only soft bounces. It stops wasted retries on missing mailboxes and leaves more of the hourly cap for soft bounces. It costs new bounce-parsing code in `mailer/bounces.py`. It becomes the primary when soft bounces need more than 4 retries to clear.
