**Ruling recorded.** The project has no decision register or handoff file, so here is the ruling in one line: "`invoice-mailer` sends from `invoices@mail.larkspur.io`, whose SPF record lists `smtp-mx-2`." Item 1 of 3 is closed. The cutover date for `smtp-old-1` follows after this item.

Item 2: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT = 12` in `mailer/config.py` gets `invoices@mail.larkspur.io` blocked by `smtp-mx-2`. Aelita recommends lowering it to 4.

**Timeline without the fix**

1. Oskar Brandt's mailbox does not exist. `invoice-mailer` sends INV-2207 through `smtp-mx-2`, and bounce 1 comes back.
2. `handle_bounce` sees attempt 0, below the limit of 12. It sleeps 60 seconds and resends.
3. Bounces 2 to 4 follow at 60-second steps. Each one triggers a resend.
4. Bounce 5 arrives with attempt 4. `handle_bounce` resends again.
5. Bounce 6 arrives about 5 minutes in. `smtp-mx-2` blocks the sender for 24 hours, because one recipient bounced more than 5 times in one hour.
6. `handle_bounce` keeps retrying until attempt 12, and every retry fails. All invoices from `invoices@mail.larkspur.io` wait 24 hours.

**Timeline with the fix**, diverges at step 4.

4. Bounce 5 arrives with attempt 4. `handle_bounce` meets the limit of 4 and calls `message.mark_failed()`.
5. `smtp-mx-2` counts 5 bounces, which is not more than 5. The sender stays unblocked.
6. Other invoices go out normally. INV-2207 is marked failed.

**What changes**

- `mailer/config.py`: `BOUNCE_RETRY_LIMIT` changes from `12` to `4`.
- `mailer/bounces.py`: no change. `handle_bounce` already compares `attempt` to the limit.

**Recommendation.** Aelita recommends limit 4. The cause is that the limit of 12 was tuned for `smtp-old-1`, which has no cap, and one value change fixes it. The limit of 4 gives 5 sends and 5 bounces per message, the most `smtp-mx-2` allows. The cost is that a soft bounce gets only 4 retries over about 4 minutes. It also leaves no margin. A second invoice that bounces for the same recipient within the hour still triggers the block.

**Alternative: classify hard and soft bounces.** `handle_bounce` fails a hard bounce at once and retries a soft bounce up to 4 times. A dead mailbox then costs one bounce, not five, and the margin returns. It needs new code, because `docs/mail-provider.md` says `invoice-mailer` cannot tell the two apart today. It becomes the primary if recipients with dead mailboxes get several invoices per hour.
