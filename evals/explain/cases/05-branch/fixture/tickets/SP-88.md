# SP-88: Duplicate purchase orders on 22 October

Reporter: Tomas Lindqvist (procurement)

Supplier Halvorsen Tools received two identical purchase orders for SKU HT-4410 on 22 October.
Both orders came from the nightly `reorder` job of `restock-planner`.
Two `restock-planner` pods ran `reorder` that night. The first pod started at 01:00. The second pod started at 01:02, after a node restart.
The job ran for about 4 minutes on each pod.
Region: unknown. Both regions run `restock-planner` and both regions buy from Halvorsen Tools.
