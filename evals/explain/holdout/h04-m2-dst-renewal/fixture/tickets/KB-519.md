# KB-519: double renewal charges on 25 October

Reporter: Mira Kowalczyk, Customer Support

- 212 customers see two identical renewal charges dated 25 October on their card statements.
- Several customers also received the dunning reminder twice that night.
- On Monday 26 October, support refunded some of the double charges by hand from the Payrail dashboard. Nobody kept a list of which customers got a refund.
